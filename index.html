Montana - Big Sky Country

A page about the state of Montana

Montana officially became the 41st U.S. state on November 8, 1889

The name "Montana" comes from the Spanish word montaña meaning "mountain" or "mountainous country", which originates from the Latin word montanea or montanus

Three facts about Montana

  • Montana is the 4th-largest U.S. state by area.
  • Cows Outnumber People: There are roughly two to three cows for every human resident in the state.
  • Montana is called "Big Sky Country" becuase of its vast, open landscapes and expansive, unobstructed horizons that make the sky feel massive.
